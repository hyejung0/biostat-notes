21st Kolokotrones Symposium held by CAUSALab at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health on April 3, 2026. The symposium focuses on the use of external controls in causal inference, covering topics such as study design and analysis methods.

1 Symposium Info

21st Kolokotrones Symposium, External Controls for Causal Inference: Study Design and Analysis. CAUSALab event in Collaboration with Richard A. and Susan F. Smith Center for Outcomes Research at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center.

Moderated by Issa Dahabreh (Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health) and Robert Yeh (Richard A. and Susan F. Smith Center for Outcomes Research).

The panel will be moderated by Christina Lalani (Richard A. and Susan F. Smith Center for Outcomes Research) and Dhruv Kazi (Richard A. and Susan F. Smith Center for Outcomes Research).

5 Issa Dahabreh

creating smaller trial from an existing trial.

We shouldn’t think we can get model correctly. So we try to learn from the data. But to learn model from the data, I need large data…. But I need to get the model to gain efficiency. So how do we solve this issue?

external controls requires very strong assumption.

But the hybrid/fusion design… randomization in smaller trial can get by without any assumption…?

He personally doesn’t feel very lucky.

  1. take chance: rare disease or randomization is not possible
  2. Use design: get external non-experimental data.
  3. When I can use randomization in smaller sample size, he prefers that.
